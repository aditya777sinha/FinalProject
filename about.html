About RentIt

RentIt is a student-friendly rental platform designed for university hostel students.

Our goal is to make everyday items easily available to students without the need to buy everything.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an affordable and convenient way for students to rent, use and return everyday items.

What We Offer

  • Daily-use items for rent
  • Affordable prices for students
  • Easy item browsing
  • Simple rental booking
  • A platform for students to list their items

Why Choose RentIt?

Students often need certain items only for a short period of time. Instead of buying those items, they can rent them from other students at an affordable price.
